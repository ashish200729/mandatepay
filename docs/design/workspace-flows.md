# Workspace flows

Mode: Operate. Scope: chat, discovery, mandates, approvals, checkout, receipts, refunds, and settings. Preserve the approved landing identity and backend financial contracts. The dashboard ledger redesign remains in place.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One clear task at a time, with permissions and the next action close to the record.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory workspace, sand selection and permission accents, white records, warm borders, Hedvig page headings, Satoshi controls and data.

STORY: Describe a need, select permissions, compare options, review the exact proposal, complete checkout, and inspect or refund the purchase.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Chat has a compact title and mandate bar, an independently scrolling conversation, and a composer anchored below it. Other pages open with a compact heading and task-specific controls.

FORM: A conversation workspace; a separate discovery flow; describe/review/activate mandate steps; an approval inbox beside its selected record; readable receipt and refund history.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
